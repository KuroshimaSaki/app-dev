# Jujustsu Kaisen

**Description** 
The story of Jujutsu Kaisen follows high school student Yuji Itadori, who becomes a vessel for the powerful and feared curse, Ryomen Sukuna, after swallowing one of his cursed fingers to save his friends.  

_Genre:_ Dark Fantasy, Action

**Characters**
- Yuji Itadori
- Kugisaki Nobarra
- Gojo Satorou
- Megumi Fushiguro
- Sukuna
- Maki Zenin
- Toge Inumaki 
- Exciting plot twists
